# STORYBOOK limits (say kindly when a wish is outside them, offer the closest thing)

- **Cast:** paper puppets with jointed arms and legs on pins — no fingers, no lip-sync (mouth shapes for smile / open /
  sad / flat), no turning around. People: child / adult / elder, 8 hair styles, 5 outfits, colours, dots / stripes,
  glasses, beard. Animals: cat, rabbit, dog, bird, duck, fish, butterfly, living star — no other animals (a horse, an
  elephant, a dragon) without development work; offer the nearest one or keep it in the words.
- **Actions:** the verb list in story-format.md, one per character per page. No fighting, dancing, climbing, eating,
  carrying another character, riding, driving.
- **Up to 5 characters** on the ground per page; crowds are not drawn.
- **Places:** garden with a house, park, village, forest, beach, sea, mountains, rice fields, river, bedroom, school,
  town, open sky. Other places (a market, a mosque, a palace, a space ship) map to the nearest one; a specific building
  is development work.
- **Props:** the ~40 library pieces; vehicles (car, bus, train, plane) are not in the library.
- **Book:** every story page is a right-hand page (the left page facing it is printed paper with a small flourish; in 16:9 it carries the page's text);
  one page turn per page; 4–12 pages (more makes a long video). Text printed under the picture, about 4 short lines
  per page at most.
- **Language:** Indonesian text (fonts include Latin accents; no Arabic script).
- **Voice:** the buyer's VO, or a temporary macOS voice (Damayanti) only after approval; no dialogue voices.
- **Sound:** an original synthesised music-box lullaby and paper / story sounds; no licensed music, no vocals.
- **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920 @ 60 fps (preview 540×960 @ 30) by default. 16:9 only when the buyer asks, with
  `--wide`: 1920×1080 @ 60 (preview 960×540 @ 30) from the same naskah and VO — the open book as a two-page spread,
  the page's text large on the left page (on the first spread under the title and dedication), the picture on the
  right page. In 16:9 a close-up is taken only when the action fits a low frame (one or two characters on the ground,
  a flier, a swimmer, a sleeper); pages with three or more characters or a kite stay on the spread. Keep the first
  page ≤ ~20 words when the title or dedication is long. No 1:1 / 4:5.
- **Platform:** tested on macOS Apple Silicon with Node 24; Node ≥ 22.18 required; `--tts` needs macOS.
